ℹ️ Method

TAL-TADscore computes a Composite Activation Score based on five biophysical properties associated with transcriptional activation domain function:

S = 0.30 × acidic_density + 0.25 × LFW_content + 0.20 × neg_charge + 0.15 × interleaving + 0.10 × motif_identity

The Sequence Logo displays per-position activation relevance: letter height reflects the residue's contribution to the composite score. The Optimized Variant applies greedy single-position substitutions to maximize the score while preserving overall fold compatibility.

Weights derived from Random Forest feature importance analysis of 126 unique CTR sequences across five Xanthomonas species (Donayre-Torres & Gutierrez-Pescarmona, 2026).
